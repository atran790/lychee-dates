Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Overview

Lychee: Date Planner ("the App") is published by Pomegranate Planning, LLC ("we", "us"). There's no account, and your photos stay on your iPhone. To plan your dates, the App sends the tastes you keep and your plan settings to our server and on to OpenAI. It also sends anonymous usage events so we can make the App better. Both are described below.

Photos

With your permission, the App looks at your photo library on your iPhone with Apple's on-device tools (and Apple Intelligence on iPhones that have it) to suggest things you two enjoy. Photos and what's found in them are never uploaded. To name the places you go, the App sends Apple Maps the map coordinates of up to about 30 places you photograph often, plus a rough location to find your city; Apple handles these under Apple's privacy policy. You choose which suggestions to keep.

Date Planning with OpenAI

The App tells you this under the Plan button before your first plan. When you plan or change a date night, including weekly dates, our server sends OpenAI the tastes you kept, your city and areas, your plan settings, anything you typed, and the current plan when you ask for a change. It never sends your photos. OpenAI searches the web and suggests places, under its API terms: it doesn't use this data to train its models and keeps it for up to 30 days to monitor abuse. See OpenAI's privacy policy.

Plans are written by AI from public listings and can be wrong, so check hours and prices before you go. Don't type anything you wouldn't want used for planning, such as health details.

What We Store

Our server (Supabase) stores your kept tastes, city, settings, plans, favorites and any problem reports, linked to a random ID for this install, so the App can show them again and run weekly dates. If you turn on weekly dates, it also stores your time zone and a notification token so Apple can deliver the Thursday notification. The App uses Apple's App Attest to prove requests come from the real App. We keep this data until you ask us to delete it.

Analytics and Advertising

The App sends anonymous usage events to PostHog, our analytics provider, to learn which features help and where people get stuck: for example which screen opened, that a scan or plan finished and how long it took, plus general device and App details (model, iOS version, language, time zone, App version). Each install gets a random ID; deleting the App resets it. Events never include photos, the tastes you kept, or anything you type. PostHog is told not to keep your IP address. We don't sell data, share it with advertisers or track you across other companies' apps, and the App has no ads.

If You Live Outside the United States

The App works the same everywhere. Our server, OpenAI and PostHog may process data in the United States. Under laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation, you can contact us with any privacy question or request, including to see or delete your data, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Your Choices and Deleting Your Data

You can turn off weekly dates in the App's Settings, and photo or notification access in your iPhone's Settings. To have the data on our server and PostHog deleted, email us with the ID shown in the App's Settings. Deleting the App removes what's stored on your phone.

Children's Privacy

The App is for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at anh@withlychee.com.
